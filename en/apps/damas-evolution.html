About the app

Damas Evolution is the Brazilian checkers game for mobile, built with Flutter. The rules are the official ones: mandatory capture under the majority rule, backward capture, flying kings, and a draw after 40 moves without progress.

Face an AI with three difficulty levels — Easy, Medium, and Hard — or use Player vs Player mode for local matches with a friend. Choose between the 2D and 3D board, with animations and sound effects, and track your progress in the match history and statistics.

Available in Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, and Italian, with accessibility features and layouts that adapt from small phones to tablets, in portrait and landscape.
