About the app

MY CAR LAS is a Flutter app for anyone who wants to know what their car or motorbike really costs and what it needs — with no spreadsheet and no internet required. You log fuel-ups, expenses, maintenance and mileage, and the app turns those records into average consumption, a history and a monthly report.

Reminders are the heart of the app: oil changes, insurance, inspection, taxes and fees, or a mileage update. They can fall due by date, by mileage or whichever comes first, repeat on their own and warn you in advance with a local notification, with no server involved.

The data lives in a SQLite database on the device itself, and the app asks for no account and sends nothing to the internet. Under the hood it uses Clean Architecture with MVVM, Riverpod, Drift and automated tests with a coverage requirement. The project is in active development, for Android and Windows; iOS comes later.
