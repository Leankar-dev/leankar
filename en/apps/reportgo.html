About the app

ReportGoApp is a Flutter app for creating technical photo reports — inspections, appraisals, site surveys and expert reports — right from the work site. The tagline sums up the idea: "Register. We do the rest."

You create the report, shoot or import the images, describe each one, collect the signature and export the PDF. The app takes care of numbering, metadata, organization and protecting the report once it is signed.

Everything works offline and your data stays on your device. The only exception is the map, which needs to download map imagery when a connection is available. A cloud version (backup and sync) is planned as a future optional feature.
