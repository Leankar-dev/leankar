Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the leankar.dev website collects, uses, and protects visitors' information, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD). By browsing this site, you acknowledge that you are aware of the terms described below.

01

Who we are

The leankar.dev website is the personal portfolio of Leandro, a mobile developer specialized in Flutter and Dart, with apps published on Google Play. The site showcases projects, technical articles, a development stack, and a professional contact channel.

Data controller: Leandro (leankar.dev)
Email: leankar.dev@gmail.com
Website: leankar.dev
02

Data collected automatically

When you access any page on this site, integrated third-party services may automatically collect the information below. We do not collect personal data directly ourselves.

  • Google Analytics 4 — ID: G-8V438JPJTN: collects browsing data in pseudonymized form, including pages visited, session duration, country of origin, device type, and browser. The IP address is anonymized by Google before storage.
03

Contact form

The contact form is processed by the Formspree service (formspree.io). When you submit the form, the following data is collected:

  • Name — required field
  • Email — required field, used to reply
  • Message — required field

The data is transmitted via HTTPS to Formspree's servers and forwarded to leankar.dev@gmail.com. It is not stored in any database of our own. See Formspree's Privacy Policy for more details.

Purpose: responding to professional contact requests.
Legal basis (LGPD): legitimate interest (Art. 7, IX) and performance of a contract at the data subject's request (Art. 7, V).
04

Use of cookies

This site only uses third-party cookies for audience analysis. We do not use our own cookies.

  • Google Analytics (_ga, _gid, _ga_*): identify unique sessions and track browsing behavior in aggregate form. They expire after up to 2 years (_ga) or 24 hours (_gid).

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings.

06

Legal bases for data processing (LGPD)

As required by the LGPD (Art. 7), the legal bases for each processing activity are listed below:

  • Google Analytics: the controller's legitimate interest in audience analysis and site improvement (Art. 7, IX), with pseudonymized data and active IP anonymization.
  • Contact form (Formspree): performance of procedures at the data subject's request (Art. 7, V) and legitimate interest in handling professional inquiries (Art. 7, IX).
07

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer personal data to third parties for commercial purposes. The only third parties that receive data are the service operators described in this policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy — Analytics, Fonts

  • ViaCEP — CEP lookups by the BuscaCep_las app (public API, no personal data logged)
08

Your rights (LGPD — Art. 18)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Confirm that processing exists and obtain access to the processed data.

Correction

Request the update of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data.

Deletion

Of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in violation of the LGPD.

Portability

Request the transfer of your data to another service provider.

Objection

To processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest, in case of a violation of the LGPD.

Revocation

Of consent at any time and free of charge, for processing based on it.

Information

About the third parties with whom the data has been shared.

ANPD

File complaints with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority at gov.br/anpd.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at leankar.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe set forth by the LGPD.

10

Security

This site is served exclusively over HTTPS and does not store personal data on its own servers. Every page implements a Content Security Policy (CSP) to mitigate the risk of malicious content injection (XSS). Contact form data is transmitted end-to-end encrypted via TLS.

  • Formspree: data retained on Formspree's servers according to its policy; the site owner keeps the emails only for as long as necessary to respond to each request.
